# Multi-Asset Income & Resilience Engine

*An educational modeling tool for exploring retirement income concepts*

Explore how retirement income strategies *could* behave over time with an interactive educational modeling platform built to help you visualize, stress-test, and better understand long-term portfolio dynamics. Designed for students of personal finance, self-directed learners, and anyone who wants to build intuition around multi-asset income planning, this platform turns abstract concepts — compounding, sequence-of-returns risk, drawdown mechanics — into a hands-on, exploratory simulation.

> **This tool is for educational and illustrative purposes only. It does not provide financial, investment, tax, or legal advice, and nothing it produces should be treated as a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any security or to adopt any particular retirement strategy. All outputs are hypothetical, based on user-supplied and historical assumptions, and are not predictive of future results.**

## Key Features & What They Help You Learn

| Educational Capability | Core Functionality | What You Can Explore |
|---|---|---|
| Dynamic Multi-Asset Model | Lets you build a hypothetical mix of Primary Income, Secondary Cash Flow, and Equity Growth asset classes. | Illustrates how compounding and yield allocation *might* interact under different assumptions — a sandbox for understanding mechanics, not a portfolio recommendation. |
| Live Market Metrics Integration | Pulls reference data via yfinance (TTM returns, distribution yields, implied growth) to seed your assumptions. | Helps ground hypothetical models in real historical data points, so you can see how a model reacts when inputs change — not a signal to act on current market conditions. |
| Scenario & Stress-Test Exploration | Lets you simulate illustrative macro scenarios — early bear markets, stagflation, prolonged stagnation, fund liquidation events. | Builds intuition for how a *hypothetical* portfolio structure might respond to adverse conditions. These are simplified educational scenarios, not forecasts or guarantees of real-world performance. |
| Monte Carlo Simulation | Runs up to 10,000 stochastic simulation paths to generate a distribution of hypothetical outcomes. | Demonstrates the *concept* of probabilistic ranges (10th/50th/90th percentile paths) instead of a single deterministic projection — useful for learning why single-point forecasts can be misleading. |
| Configurable Living-Expense Rules | Adjustable income-floor triggers, cost-of-living escalators, and siphon-pause logic during drawdowns. | Helps you study, conceptually, how withdrawal rules and sequence-of-returns risk can interact — a teaching tool for these tradeoffs, not a withdrawal strategy endorsement. |
| Reporting & Export | Streamlit interface with full, formatted openpyxl Excel export. | Makes it easy to review, annotate, and discuss your hypothetical scenarios with a teacher, mentor, or licensed advisor. |

## Why Use This Platform?

- **Learn by Experimenting:** Adjust assumptions and immediately see how a hypothetical model responds — a low-stakes way to build financial modeling literacy.
- **Understand Risk Concepts, Not Just Numbers:** Purpose-built to make abstract ideas like sequence-of-returns risk and drawdown resilience tangible and visual.
- **Transparent, User-Driven Assumptions:** Every simulation is only as good as the inputs you choose — the tool is explicit about the fact that it reflects your assumptions, not market certainties or professional recommendations.

**No outcome shown by this tool is guaranteed, implied, or predictive.** Markets are unpredictable, historical data does not guarantee future performance, and this platform cannot account for your complete personal financial situation. Always consult a qualified, licensed financial advisor, tax professional, or attorney before making any actual financial or investment decisions.
